# FluxPay

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

AdonisJS 7, React/Inertia, PostgreSQL, Redis, Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui (Radix).

## Users

Administrators and collection operators at service SMEs who need to see receivables, act on overdue charges, and record payments quickly.

## Product Purpose

FluxPay centralizes recurring and ad-hoc receivables, payment registration, delinquency visibility, and localized payment reminders for multiple isolated companies.

## Positioning

An operational collection cockpit that turns open receivables into a clear daily action queue without forcing a separate frontend or public API.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multi-company isolation, platform admin, company admin and operator roles.
- Customers may be people or companies, with general and finance contacts.
- BRL and other ISO currencies are supported but are never converted or co-aggregated.
- Interface and e-mails support pt, en, es, fr and it.
- Client portal, WhatsApp, Pix, Pix Automático, settlements, interest, exchange and self-service subscriptions are out of MVP scope.

## Brand Commitments

The approved desktop dashboard preview is the visual authority: white data canvas, dark navy sidebar, strong blue action color, discrete borders, compact financial density and clear red/green status signals.

## Evidence on Hand

Approved dashboard preview: `previews/01-central-de-acao.png`.

## Product Principles

- Make the next collection action obvious.
- Preserve financial correctness over visual convenience.
- Keep every company’s data isolated by default.
- Communicate in the operator’s and customer’s chosen language.
